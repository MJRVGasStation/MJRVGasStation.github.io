About Us

Your Trusted Partner
on Every Journey

MJRV Gas Station opened on March 16, 2025, with one mission: to end the long "mapan jay ili" — the tiring trip to town just to fuel your motor. Now you can fill up right here, for as low as ₱10, with honestly measured liters and a smile. We earn just a tiny profit because we believe no one gets left behind when we help each other. Alone, you can go far. Together, we go farther — without leaving your neighborhood. — MJRV, fuel with heart, not with markup.
