Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

At MJRV Gas Station, your privacy is a priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect any personal information you provide when visiting our website or contacting us through our online forms.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information when you voluntarily submit it through our contact form or Messenger:

  • Name – to address you properly
  • Email address – to respond to your inquiries
  • Message content – to understand and assist you better

We do not collect sensitive payment or financial data through this website. Fuel transactions are processed on‑site at our physical station.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely for the following purposes:

  • Responding to your messages, questions, or service requests
  • Improving our customer service based on feedback
  • Internal record keeping (messages may be archived for quality assurance)

We will never sell, distribute, or lease your personal information to third parties unless we are required by law to do so.

3. Data Security

We are committed to ensuring that your information is secure. Although communication via the internet is not 100% guaranteed, we implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external platforms like Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze, and Facebook Messenger. Once you leave our site, we do not have control over the privacy practices of those third-party services. Please review their respective privacy policies.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our main website (index.html) does not use persistent cookies or tracking scripts. However, temporary session storage may be used for functional purposes (such as displaying fuel prices). No personal data is stored or tracked beyond your immediate visit.

6. Your Rights

You may request details of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete any messages you previously sent by contacting us through our Facebook page or Messenger. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Contacting Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please reach out:

💬 Messenger: Chat with us
📘 Facebook: MJRV Gas Station

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page. Please check back occasionally for any changes.
